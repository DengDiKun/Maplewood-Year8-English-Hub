Vocabulary support

sewers — underground pipes that carry waste water
colossal — extremely large
clump — a compact group or mass
the Shard — a very tall London building used as a size comparison
spew — flow or throw out forcefully
chunks — large pieces
around the clock — continuously, day and night

Key monster imagery to find in the article

“monster fatbergs”“greasy giants”“armed with power tools”“tackled”“monsters from the deep”“lurking and growing under our feet”“defeat these two”

Activity 2 — Writer’s effect

Write one paragraph explaining how the writer makes the fatbergs seem like monsters and what effect this has on the reader.

Choose one or two phrases. Explain how the monster/battle language makes the fatbergs seem dangerous, alive or difficult to defeat. Then explain how this affects the reader.
Teacher model idea: the extended metaphor presents the fatbergs as dangerous monsters and makes the work of removing them sound like a battle. Students do not need to copy this wording.
