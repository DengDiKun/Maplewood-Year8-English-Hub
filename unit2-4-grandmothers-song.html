Recovered extract lines

“Granddaughter dropped to her knees.”
“Grief struck as quickly and precisely as lightning.”
“She felt her heart flutter and her stomach churn…”
“She trembled from head to toe, like cedar branches in a raging storm.”
“Her heart opened like a river and tears soaked her face…”
“She doubled over in despair and sobs welled up from her belly and her bones.”

The later ending includes the ideas:

“Grandmother’s spirit is all around us.”
“She is in the wind and in the trees.”
“She is in the valleys and the hills.”
“We can simply shut our eyes and feel her holding us so very close.”

Interactive analysis

Look for lightning, cedar branches/storm and river imagery.
Do not only name the technique. Explain why the natural image is powerful for showing grief.
Either interpretation can work if you explain it with evidence from the ending.
