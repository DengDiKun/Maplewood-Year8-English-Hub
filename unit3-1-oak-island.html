Oak Island Mystery

This lesson is based on the earlier Page 65 material already shared for Unit 3.1.

60-minute teacher plan

5 minTreasure hook
8 minMap/context
10 minVocabulary
15 minGuided reading
17 minInteractive task
5 minClosure

Read the text

Oak Island is in Nova Scotia, Canada. For more than 200 years, people believe that a valuable treasure is hidden there.

Many treasure hunters travelled to the island. They dug deep holes and spent a lot of money, but they did not find anything.

Some people believe pirates buried treasure there. Others think it could be gold or jewellery.

There are also strange clues. Workers found wooden platforms underground.

Even though no treasure has been found, people are still fascinated and continue searching.

treasure — valuable objects or money
hidden — kept where it cannot easily be seen
hunters — people searching for something
dug — made a hole in the ground
buried — put under the ground
clues — pieces of information that help solve a mystery
underground — below the surface of the ground
fascinated — extremely interested

Activity

Look for information that is stated as true in the passage, such as location, time, searches or discoveries.
Think about what people still do not know for certain.
Use the final paragraph and the strange clues.
